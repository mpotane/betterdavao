# Get Free Check-ups, Basic Medicines and Vaccines — Davao City

The **City Health Office (CHO)** makes health services accessible to Davao City residents through its main office on Magallanes Street and the city's **health centers**, which serve all **182 barangays**.

---

## 1) Services the CHO Offers

- Medical consultations and check-ups
- Immunization
- Prenatal and post-partum care
- Nutrition
- Laboratory
- Dental
- Premarital counselling
- Adolescent and youth health
- Family planning
- Reproductive health and wellness (STI, HIV and AIDS)
- Counseling and referral
- Non-communicable disease services (prevention of blindness, hearing, mental health, PWDs, senior citizens) and cardio-vascular diseases
- Animal Bite and Treatment Center
- Health certificates and medical certificates

---

## 2) Free Vaccines

The CHO offers **free vaccines for all ages**, from pregnancy to birth and seniors. They include vaccines for tetanus, BCG, pentavalent, polio, flu and pneumococcal diseases, and are available at the health centers in all 182 barangays. If you miss a scheduled dose, you can still go to your health provider to receive it.

See [maternal care and child immunization](/access-maternal-care-and-child-immunization).

---

## 3) How to Avail

1. Go to the health center that serves your barangay, or to the CHO main office on Magallanes Street.
2. Tell the staff which service you need.
3. Ask which documents to bring and what the schedule is. Services and schedules can vary by health center.

The CHO's published pages do not list a free-medicines program or a complete schedule of services per health center. Ask your health center or call the CHO.
